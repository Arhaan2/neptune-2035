# Phase 8 verification summary and supported claims

**Asset-linked design-stage offshore infrastructure twin with coupled operation and failure simulation.**

**Simulated, design-stage prototype; physical validation pending.** The supported use is investigating modeled equipment dependencies, operation, disturbances, recovery and bounded design comparisons under explicit assumptions. The evidence establishes software behavior and numerical agreement within implemented model boundaries. It does not establish an identified real installation, commissioning, physical accuracy, certification, marine stability/mooring/environment/permitting, AI training throughput, statistical reliability or a globally optimal design.

Phase 8 extends existing verification machinery with a machine-readable numerical runner, matched-time three-resolution timestep observations, component and aggregate conservation measures, complete-run signature/replay checks, and checksum-bound reproduction of the six original decision campaigns. It retains the Phase 4–7 experiments, worker lifecycle, provenance, inspection, accessibility and compatibility coverage. Historical expected outputs are recovered from the immutable Phase 7 evidence release; they are never refreshed from the candidate.

The independent review found a reporting defect: equal and opposite module residuals could disappear from the signed aggregate shown in engineering reports. The small production repair retains existing signed fields and adds absolute sums, maxima and per-component normalized maxima. Separate before/after witnesses and independently authored tests prove that opposing errors and a large neighboring load cannot hide the component error. This reporting repair changes no solver equation, tolerance or event timing. The stale model-card header was also corrected. A separate independent numerical audit then found V8-05, a counterflow exchanger cancellation defect just outside the near-equal capacity branch; its correction requires numerical solver version `2.3.1` and explicit compatibility handling. The prior `2.3.0` evidence remains historical, with no silently relabeled expected results.

For V8-05, the original solver returned 10000.000045929364 W where independent spatial RK4 and 70-digit Decimal arithmetic agreed on 10000.0000275000002227 W (outside-branch absolute acceptance tolerance 1e-6 W). The changed denominator evaluates the same mathematical equation stably. Previous 2.3.0 projects remain inspectable and exportable under their original identity; exact resume through the corrected solver is unavailable. Explicit recalculation creates a separate current-model experiment and records parent provenance. Known historical identity validation retains structural, event, state and metric binding checks; it does not broadly accept mismatched hashes. Old decision imports remain explicitly version-rejected, with the bounded historical runner separately reproducing the original solver and deriving a current campaign from unchanged physical inputs. Cross-version numerical agreement and same-version exact replay are distinct evidence claims.

Actual-browser compatibility testing also found a recalculation defect: rebuilding a saved five-second checkpoint with a future eight-second event incorrectly gave the new experiment a five-second horizon. The targeted UI repair validates the derived definition before replacing the active session, preserves the original physical horizon, inputs and future events, and replays only to the selected saved time. The new run explicitly identifies its current-model cold recalculation and parent evidence. Browser acceptance must verify the intermediate fault, later recovery, original export and unrelated active campaign preservation.

The five original Python benchmarks remain an independent reference: rational battery/HX arithmetic, closed-form quadratic pump intersection, rectangular displacement identity and an independent RK4 thermal integration. New checks cover battery boundaries and zero ratios through the retained suites, supported pump counts/speeds, both exchanger streams/near-equal and signed/zero limits, exact discrete network demand, and production h=1/0.5/0.25 s comparisons. Component signed totals, sums of absolute errors and maxima are retained in W with explicit normalization. Equivalent series flow equality and algebraic network auxiliary accounting are identified as structural identities, not arbitrary network or physical validation.

Complete-run experiments retain unmet accelerator-seconds, interruption, temperature extrema and violation duration, controller history, recovery onset/dwell confirmation and explicit not-recovered/timeout outcomes. Direct, chunked, restored and replayed physical states and whole-run evidence must agree. Signature cases preserve the difference between similar final temperatures and materially different interruption histories. Transfer comparisons preserve both eligible feeder benefit and receiving-bus/common-source no-feasible outcomes. Ranking applies only to the actual candidates, constraints, included cost basis and exclusions recorded by each portable campaign.

The [original-regression ledger](ORIGINAL-REGRESSIONS.md) distinguishes the two original defects from inadequacy, assumption ownership and subsequently added capabilities. Native historical failure must be a behavioral assertion on the affected source, not an absent modern API or failed installation. Historical input hashes and output comparisons accompany the final receipt.

The [operating-envelope report](ENVELOPE.md) defines finite size/duration combinations and observed resource behavior; admission bounds do not promise equal speed at every syntactically valid size/duration combination. The two historical Chromium/WebKit exclusions concern only the named ten-second large-campus test; Firefox retains it. A separate one-second largest-campus worker case runs in all three browsers, without relabeling it as ten-second acceptance. Device-specific timings, heap observations and process RSS must remain explicitly distinct and scoped to the measured host.
